About Us

The people and the passion behind every plate.

What we do

Savour Lagos Kitchen prepares and serves traditional Nigerian meals for dine-in, takeaway, delivery and events. Our menu covers hearty mains, grilled specialities, snacks and refreshing drinks.

Our background

The restaurant began in 2012 as a small food stall run by Mrs. Adeola Bello, who cooked for neighbours and office workers. Word spread about her jollof rice, and in 2018 the family opened a full restaurant in Ikeja. Today a team of twelve cooks and servers continues her recipes.

Mission, vision and values

Our mission

To serve delicious, affordable and authentic Nigerian food prepared with fresh ingredients and care.

Our vision

To become the most trusted home-style Nigerian restaurant in Lagos, known for quality and hospitality.

Our values

  • Quality ingredients
  • Respect for tradition
  • Clean kitchens
  • Friendly service

Our journey

  1. 2012 A small food stall opens in Ikeja.
  2. 2018 The first Savour Lagos Kitchen restaurant opens.
  3. 2021 Delivery service and event catering begin.
  4. 2025 Over 50,000 plates served to happy guests.
